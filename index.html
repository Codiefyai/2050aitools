Data Management

Manage your application data.

IMPORTANT: Currently, data (Leads, Calls, Appointments, Reports) is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It is NOT automatically synced with your user account in Supabase or across different browsers/devices. Clearing your browser data WILL erase everything.

To make data user-specific and cloud-synced, the application's `loadDataFromStorage` and `saveDataToStorage` functions need to be rewritten to interact with your Supabase database tables instead of localStorage.


Warning: Clearing local data cannot be undone. Local backups are stored locally and will also be cleared if you manually clear browser site data. Use Export buttons in each section to save data externally.
